Research & Publications

I work on computational geometry and verifiable optimization problems. My current research program, Ringmin, studies the minimum central-circle problem: arranging circles of radii 1,2,...,n around a central circle while minimizing its radius.

Publications

Paper I arXiv:2607.28654 2026

Arranging circles of radii 1,2,...,n around a central circle: a Supnick TSP and certified finite optima

The finite paper reduces the chain-ordering lower-bound problem to a fixed Supnick/anti-Monge TSP and is accompanied by explicit certificate artifacts and an independent verifier. The repository certifies global optima for n = 3,...,14; larger-n candidate optima remain heuristic.

Paper II arXiv:2609.13630 2026

Minimum central circles: an effective characterization of the global asymptotic constant

The asymptotic sequel proves R*(n) = C* n² + o(n²) and characterizes C* through finite balanced-word linear programs with a rigorous error bound that vanishes as the finite parameters grow. An elementary closed form and an efficient evaluation algorithm remain open.
